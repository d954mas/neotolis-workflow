# TB-06 — Phase ownership, blockers, and interruption

## Goal

Implement shared state-changing phase authority for `nttask`.

## Dependencies

- `TB-05` committed and passing.

## Owned files

- `src/runtime/phase.ts`
- phase-related integration in `src/cli/main.ts`
- `tests/phase/**`

## Implementation contract

- Add `phase begin nttask`, `phase complete nttask`, and `phase stop nttask` only.
- Accept only canonical `claude:<native-id>` and `codex:<native-id>` owners.
- Begin records `phase` and `owner` only at a legal lifecycle boundary.
- Only the exact recorded owner may complete or normally stop.
- Replacement requires `provider-ended` or `user-confirmed` interruption authority.
- A blocker prevents begin unless `--blocker-resolved` accompanies explicit resolution.
- Controlled `nttask` stop requires a non-empty blocker and clears phase/owner without lifecycle advancement.
- Complete requires matching owner and `blocker:null`.
- Unexpected interruption leaves ownership recorded.

## Acceptance

`TB-AC-06` is complete when owner conflicts, takeover, blocker resolution, stop, and complete paths preserve all authority boundaries.
